About the Store

Abbottabad shops here

Day to Day Shopping Arcade is Abbottabad's biggest ready-made garments store — a full four-floor arcade on Mansehra Road in Mirpur where families from across Hazara come to dress everyone in a single trip.

From a father's kurta shalwar to a mother's embroidered suit and the children's Eid outfits, every floor is stocked with ready-to-wear clothing in every size and budget — no stitching wait, just pick, try and take home.

  • Ready-made garments — no tailoring wait
  • Gents, ladies & kids departments under one roof
  • Easy to reach on N-35, near Mirpur Police Station
  • Fresh stock arriving every week
